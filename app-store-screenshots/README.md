## 📤 How to Upload to App Store Connect

1. **Log in to App Store Connect**
   - Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
   - Navigate to "My Apps" → Select PocketTrack

2. **Upload iPhone Screenshots**
   - Click on your app version
   - Scroll to "App Preview and Screenshots"
   - Find "6.5-inch Display" section
   - Drag and drop all 5 files from `iphone-6.5/` folder
   - Apple will automatically scale these for other iPhone sizes

3. **Upload iPad Screenshots**
   - In the same section, find "13-inch iPad Display"
   - Drag and drop all 5 files from `ipad-13/` folder
   - Apple will automatically scale these for other iPad sizes
